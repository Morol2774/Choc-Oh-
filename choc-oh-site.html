why
chocoh!
It started on a kitchen counter — a little girl watching her mother bake birthday cakes, and cleaning the bowl afterwards.
That love of chocolate led Delna Patel to train and work in France, before bringing it all home to Toronto.
In 2020, when the world stood still, she opened Choc Oh! — a place built around celebrations, cravings and small moments of joy.
Everything is still made by hand, in small batches, with the best ingredients we can find.
